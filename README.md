## 🧠 What Is AI Crypto Trader?

AI Crypto Trader is a desktop trading assistant built around a built-in neural engine. It connects to major crypto exchanges, streams live market data, and produces actionable trading signals in real time. The AI model analyzes price action, volume, order book depth, and volatility patterns across multiple timeframes to identify high-probability entries and exits.

Unlike simple indicator-based bots, AI Crypto Trader adapts to changing market conditions. It learns from recent price behavior and continuously adjusts its strategy weights — making it suitable for both trending and ranging markets.

The software is designed for traders who want automation and intelligence without writing a single line of code.
